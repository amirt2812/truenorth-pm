When to ask

  • Tenants: right after a repair is finished quickly
  • Owners: after their first monthly statement
  • Agents: after a referral you handled well

Replying to reviews

Thank them by first name, mention one specific thing, and sign as Alfredo. For a negative review, respond calmly, don’t share private details, and offer to talk by phone.

Channel 2 · the classic way property managers win owners

Absentee-owner postcards

Mail owners who own a home in Hernando County but live somewhere else. They’re the most likely to need a manager. The QR code and short link lead to the Free Rental Analysis, tagged postcard / direct_mail.

List criteria (give these to the mail house or list vendor)

  • County: Hernando, FL
  • Property type: single-family homes, townhomes, and condos
  • No homestead exemption, meaning the owner doesn’t live there
  • Owner mailing address different from the property address (absentee)
  • ZIPs: 34601, 34602, 34604, 34606, 34607, 34608, 34609, 34613, 34614
  • Optional: skip owners with 10+ properties (they usually self-manage) and properties listed for sale right now

Sources: the Hernando County Property Appraiser’s public records, a list vendor, or a mail house that builds absentee-owner lists for you (many do: search “absentee owner postcard mailing”).
