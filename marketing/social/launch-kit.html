Before you start

Three things to settle first

Florida advertising rule. Social profiles and posts count as advertising, and Florida Rule 61J2-10.025 requires real-estate ads to show the licensed name of the brokerage firm. The website still shows the placeholder [Licensed Brokerage LLC Name]. Before you publish, confirm that “TrueNorth Property Management” is the name registered with DBPR, or add the registered brokerage name to every About section below (marked [Brokerage name]).

  1. Use one business login for everything.Sign up with alfredo@truenorthpm.co (not a personal Gmail) so the accounts belong to the business. Turn on two-factor authentication on each one.
  2. Decide on the street address.If 7629 Odessa Ct is a residence, set Facebook and Google up as a service-area business (hide the address, list the five towns).
  3. Create Facebook first.Instagram and LinkedIn are faster once the Facebook Page exists, and Meta Business Suite lets you schedule Facebook and Instagram posts together.

After launch

Send me the four profile links

Once the accounts exist, paste their URLs into chat. I’ll add them to src/lib/site.ts, which turns on the social icons in the website footer and adds the profiles to Google’s structured data (sameAs). Nothing shows on the site until real links are in.

Running ads later? Meta requires the Housing special ad category for rental listings and most real-estate ads. It limits age, ZIP-code, and gender targeting to comply with fair housing law, so plan audiences around it.

All graphics are in truenorth-pm/marketing/social/images/. To change the copy or colors, edit render.py in the same folder and re-run python3 marketing/social/render.py.
